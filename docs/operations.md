# Bar operations

- Existing inventory and recipes keep their user_id: it is now also the bar owner's workspace ID. Google-authenticated staff join through email-bound invitations, accepted from Team. No invitation or purchase email is sent by the application.
- Owners manage recipes, prices, suppliers, purchase orders, business settings, day closes and staff access. Staff can read reports and record sales, direct deliveries, stocktakes and waste, and receive existing purchase orders. Staff cannot overwrite inventory details, modify recipes/costs, close days or grant access. Profiles and preferred units remain personal.
- Sales capture an immutable ingredient-cost estimate when saved. A recipe must have ingredients and every ingredient needs bottle/pack capacity and purchase price. Zero price is valid. Previously saved sales and manual items without a recipe remain uncosted; no historical backfill is fabricated.
- Contribution is revenue on costed sales minus those saved ingredient costs. It excludes operating expenses and is not net profit. Current inventory valuation uses the current saved unit cost, is incomplete when prices are missing, and is not FIFO or a financial accounting valuation.
- Dashboard aggregation reads all sales for the selected interval, independently of the 500-row history limit. Today/last 7 days/month-to-date/custom ranges use the bar timezone and day boundary; comparison is the immediately preceding equal-length interval.
- Business days default to Asia/Tokyo, 04:00–04:00. Before the first day close the owner can change these settings. They lock after any close to keep saved dates meaningful.
- Stocktakes snapshot expected quantities/capacities and atomically replace only selected counts. Any intervening stock or capacity change rejects the complete operation. A restart reloads current inventory and clears the draft.
- Waste has a reason, timestamp, actor and saved cost estimate. It atomically deducts stock; insufficient stock, future timestamps and closed business days reject it.
- Purchase orders preserve item names, units, capacities and prices, progress through draft/ordered/partial/received, and permit cancellation of only the remaining quantities. Partial receipts add exactly the saved order volume. Explicit owner opt-in updates the current bottle size and price for future costing; unknown order prices clear the current cost instead of inventing one.
- A day close snapshots sales/payment/cost/waste totals plus opening and counted cash. Expected cash = opening cash + recorded cash sales, without unrecorded cash withdrawals/refunds/expenses. New sales/waste for a closed day are blocked. Owners can reopen with a reason; the original snapshot remains immutable. Closing again creates another snapshot.
- Workspace-level locks serialize sales, waste, day closes, stocktakes and order receipts. UUID request references with content hashes make new stock workflows retry-safe; changed retries reject rather than duplicate. Stock rows are locked in stable order. Audit rows are committed in the same transaction as existing/new mutations. Old idempotent request retries may add an audit submission row without adding stock or sales again.
- Team revocation is checked on every request. A revoked active workspace rejects writes; loading state/workspace selection falls back to the user's own bar. Invites are bound to the verified Google email, expire after seven days, and are never accepted solely by knowing an invite ID.

History panels show the latest 100 stocktakes/closes and 200 orders/waste/audit rows; report totals are not limited by those panels. Browser-level interaction QA requires a signed-in session; CI covers backend workflows, production frontend build, container startup and Blueprint schema.
